Yuanshu Liu (Law) - REQ4 (Mines & Breakables / Enchanted Tools) and REQ5 (Part B: Wanderer)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I used AI in the following ways:

(i) Generate ideas or structure suggestions and assist with understanding core concepts

I used Claude (Anthropic) through the Claude Code desktop app (https://claude.ai/code)
to audit my REQ4 and REQ5 designs against SOLID, DRY, KISS and YAGNI, to explain the
assignment specification and marking rubric, and to compare design alternatives with
their respective trade-offs (for example, a hardcoded enchantment list versus an
injected enchantment strategy, and static factory methods versus constructor-injected
factories). I decided which suggestions to adopt; my earlier RuneFormation design for
REQ4 was abandoned after TA feedback and replaced with the Enchanted Tools design
submitted.

(ii) Generate and refactor code

I used Claude to refactor my REQ4 and REQ5 classes and shared classes flagged in our
SOLID audit — removing static methods and marker interfaces, tightening access
modifiers, replacing Supplier-based factories with named factory interfaces, and
injecting dependencies (AreaFactory, RandomUtils, cold/frost thresholds) instead of
duplicating them across classes — and to write unit tests and a manual fuzz-testing
harness (WandererStressHarness) for REQ5. I reviewed and tested the resulting code
and take responsibility for the correctness and quality of my submitted work.
